## Independent-review procedure

1. Distribute identical source references and each AI's instruction file.
2. Collect individual written opinions **before** showing cross-review results.
3. Require evidence, strengths, weaknesses, unresolved questions, counterarguments, alternatives and design verdicts.
4. Cross-review contradictory recommendations, preserving minority conclusions.
5. Escalate unresolved Canon questions and P0 blockers; scores cannot override these.
6. Submit recommendations to the Human Product Owner; no automatic authorization.
